## PDF or this site?

Both are drawn from the same source, but they aren't interchangeable:

- **This site** is the maintained version. It's searchable, it links related tasks together, and it's corrected as the portal changes. Use it day to day.
- **The PDF** is the formal TCAA document, laid out for print and controlled by version number. Use it when you need a fixed, citable copy.
