    Quick Onboarding with CASP¶

    Read this before registering.

    The Tanzania Civil Aviation Authority (TCAA) is implementing a programme of continual improvement aimed at modernising its business processes, operational systems and information management — enhancing service delivery, strengthening regulatory oversight, improving operational efficiency, promoting transparency and accountability, and aligning the Authority's services with national priorities and international best practices.

    As part of these initiatives, the Authority has introduced the Civil Aviation Services Portal (CASP), an integrated web-based platform that automates end-to-end regulatory business processes across various technical and operational areas. The Portal facilitates online service delivery, electronic payments, workflow and document management, automated notifications, dashboards and real-time reporting. The Authority has also established a 24-hour Customer Service Help Desk for continuous support.

    1. Account types — selecting your profile¶

    To maintain regulatory integrity and ensure accurate authority delegation, CASP separates user accounts into two categories. Choose the correct profile during registration:

    Organization AccountCorporate / Entity

    For all corporate, commercial, private, and government entities requiring regulatory authorization.

    Eligible stakeholders:

    • Commercial Air Operators & General Aviation
    • Approved Maintenance Organisations (AMOs)
    • Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs)
    • Aerodrome & Helipad Operators
    • Ground Handling Service Providers
    • Aviation Security Organisations & ATOs
    • Government Ministries, Agencies & Departments

    Mandatory rule: An authorized Account Administrator must create the entity profile first. Individual staff members must not register corporate or government entities under personal accounts.

    Individual AccountPersonal

    For individual aviation professionals applying for or managing personal licences, ratings, and permits.

    Eligible stakeholders:

    • Flight Crew (Pilots, Flight Engineers)
    • Aircraft Maintenance Engineers (AMEs)
    • Air Traffic Controllers (ATCOs)
    • Flight Operations Officers / Dispatchers
    • Aviation Medical Examiners (AMEs)
    • Individual Rating & Permit Applicants

    Personal account note: Always register using your official personal email to retain direct ownership of your professional aviation credentials.

    2. Registration prerequisites checklist¶

    1. Active & monitored email address

    All official TCAA correspondence, status updates, meeting alerts, GePG payment acknowledgments, account activation links, authentication codes, and verification OTPs are routed strictly via registered email.

    2. Valid mobile telephone number

    Required for account profile records and emergency contact details.

    3. Official registration / identity details

    Company incorporation details and TIN (for entities), or Passport/National ID and existing licence numbers (for individuals).

    4. Document file format PDF only

    All uploaded supporting documentation must strictly be in PDF format. Image files (JPG/PNG) and Word documents will be rejected.

    5. Maximum upload size 100 MB

    The total combined size of all PDF attachments for a single application must not exceed 100 MB.

    6. Stable internet connection

    A stable connection is recommended when uploading larger PDF documents, to prevent session timeout.

    3. The applicant's journey¶

    A regulatory review pipeline — from registration to certificate issuance.

    1
    Account Registration
    Register profile & complete key data fields
    2
    Electronic Submission
    Fill application form, upload PDFs, and settle applicable fees
    3
    Regulatory Review
    Compliance assessed through document evaluation, demonstration, or inspection where required
    4
    Licence Issuance
    Instant online certificate delivery
