Welcome to CASP

The Civil Aviation Services Portal (CASP) is the official single-window online platform designed by the Tanzania Civil Aviation Authority (TCAA) to streamline civil aviation regulatory services. CASP enables aviation stakeholders to submit regulatory applications, upload supporting documentation, make secure online payments, track application progress in real time, and manage all active licenses, permits, and certificates through a centralized account.

1. Account Types: Selecting Your Profile

To maintain regulatory integrity and ensure accurate authority delegation, CASP separates user accounts into two main categories. Please review the definitions below to choose the correct profile during registration:

Organization Account Corporate/Entity

Designed for all corporate, commercial, private, and government entities requiring regulatory authorization.

Eligible Stakeholders:
  • Commercial Air Operators & General Aviation
  • Approved Maintenance Organisations (AMOs)
  • Air Navigation Service Providers (ANSPs)
  • Aerodrome & Helipad Operators
  • Ground Handling Service Providers
  • Aviation Security Organisations & ATOs
  • Government Ministries, Agencies & Departments
Mandatory Rule: An authorized Account Administrator must create the entity profile first. Individual staff members must not register corporate or government entities under personal accounts.
Individual Account Personal

Designed for individual aviation professionals applying for or managing personal licenses, ratings, and permits.

Eligible Stakeholders:
  • Flight Crew (Pilots, Flight Engineers)
  • Aircraft Maintenance Engineers (AMEs)
  • Air Traffic Controllers (ATCOs)
  • Flight Operations Officers / Dispatchers
  • Aviation Medical Examiners (AMEs)
  • Individual Rating & Permit Applicants
Personal Account Note: Always register using your official personal email to retain direct ownership of your professional aviation credentials.

2. Registration Prerequisites Checklist

1. Active & Monitored Email Address

All official TCAA correspondence, status updates, meeting alerts, GePG payment acknowledgments, account activation links, authentication codes, and verification OTPs are strictly routed via registered Email.

2. Valid Mobile Telephone Number

Required for account profile records and emergency contact details.

3. Official Registration / Identity Details

Company incorporation details/TIN (for entities) or Passport/National ID & existing license numbers (for individuals).

4. Document File Format Rules PDF

All uploaded supporting documentation must strictly be in PDF format. Image files (JPG/PNG) or Word documents will be rejected.

5. Maximum Upload Size Limit allowed 100 MB

The total combined size of all PDF attachments for a single application must not exceed 100 MB.

6. Stable Internet Connection

A stable connection is recommended when uploading larger PDF documents to prevent session timeout.
